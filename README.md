 ### Key Insights From The Dashboards ###

*Supply Chain Findings*
1. *Delivery is the First issue*
`Sahlagoods SA` and `Globatrade NG` have the highest late deliveries. `Express` shipping has the best On-Time rate. `Overnight` is the worst.
2. *Damage is highly spread*
`Health & Beauty`, `Home Appliances`, and `Industrial Equipment` have the highest damaged goods.
3. *Inventory Alert*
For every category, `Order Quantity` massively exceeds `Stock Level`. Risk of stockouts is high, especially in `Health & Beauty` and `Home Appliances`.

*Performance & Profitability Findings*
1. *Revenue Drivers*: `Health & Beauty`, `Food & Beverages`, and `Home Appliances` generate the most revenue.
2. *Margin Leaders*: `Health & Beauty` and `Clothing` have the highest profit margins.
3. *Regional Performance*: `East Africa` and `Southern Africa` contribute the most to profit.
4. *Top Suppliers*: `Quickship Co`, `Afroparts Ltd`, and `Globatrade NG` are the top profit contributors.
5. *Trend*: Monthly revenue shows a gradual decline from earlier months to September.


 ### Recommendations ###
There is an action behind every Data

- *Fix Delivery*: Shift more volume to `Express` shipping. Address the Late suppliers and send more orders via express and late comers should be penalized. 
- *Reduce Damage*: Audit packaging and handling for `Health & Beauty` and `Industrial Equipment`.Monitor how handlers treat high value items. 
- *Restock Proactively*: Increase inventory for high-demand categories to close the gap between `Orders` and `Stock`. Buy more stock before running out and lose sales.
- *Invest in Winners*: Double down on high-margin categories and top-performing regions/suppliers. Invest more on Suplliers that is making the most money.
- *Monitor Trends*: Use the dashboards weekly to catch revenue drops and shipping delays early. Check how things are run everyweek so that you can fix the problem before getting big.


### Conclusion ###
Globamart doesn’t have a sales problem. It has an *operational problem*.

Fix delivery speed, inventory planning, and product quality — and that $714M revenue can turn into even more profit and happier customers.
